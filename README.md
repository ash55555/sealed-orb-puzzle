# The Sealed Orb

A one-face, Rubik's-cube-style puzzle in 3D, made as a table prop for a tabletop game. Six colored stones sit on a stone plate. Slide the rows and flip the columns until the stones match the seal picture, and the orb awakens.

**Play it:** https://ash55555.github.io/sealed-orb-puzzle/

## How to play

- The small picture under the title is the **seal**: the arrangement you need to restore.
- **Slide a row** left or right with the arrow buttons. The stone at the end wraps around to the other side.
- **Flip a column** with the up/down button to swap its two stones.
- Every color also has its own shape, so the puzzle works for color-blind players.
- Every puzzle takes at least 5 moves. When you win, the game tells you whether you used the fewest moves possible.
- Move your mouse (or finger) over the board to tilt it in 3D.

## Run it yourself

It's a single `index.html` file with no dependencies. Open it in any browser.

## Make it your own

All colors and shapes live in the `COLORS` list near the top of the script in `index.html`.
