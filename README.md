# The Sun Orb

A 3D stone-ball twist puzzle in Maya style, made as a table prop for a tabletop game. The ball is built from 24 carved stones in six colors, each with its own glyph. Turn parts of the orb until every side shows a single color and glyph, and the orb awakens.

**Play it:** https://ash55555.github.io/sealed-orb-puzzle/

## How to play

- **Turn:** press a stone and drag along the ball. Half of the orb turns with it.
- **Look around:** drag the empty space around the ball (or use the arrow keys) to spin the whole orb.
- **Goal:** every side of the ball shows one color and one glyph.
- Pick **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard** to change how scrambled the orb starts. **Undo** and **Restart** are always available.
- Every color has its own glyph, so the puzzle works for color-blind players.

## Run it yourself

It's a single `index.html` file with no dependencies. Open it in any browser.

## Make it your own

The colors and carved glyphs live at the top of the script in `index.html` (`STONES` and `GLYPHS`).
